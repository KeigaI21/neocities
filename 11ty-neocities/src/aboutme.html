About me

Hello, I'm Keiga. I make character illustrations. In my art, I attempt to capture the multitude of feelings the sun, moon, and stars evoke within all of us. I have been self-studying illustration for 4 years, and I'm proud of how much my art has grown in this time. I made this site to showcase my art in high quality and large resolution, something I can't do on Instagram. I hope you enjoy what I've made.
